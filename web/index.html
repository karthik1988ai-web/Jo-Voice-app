      Email writing

      Jo has every email written properly before you see it, then shows it in the chat for you to edit, send or save as a draft. Notes and task names are spell-checked too. Gemini is free; ChatGPT and Claude need their own API key (paid per use, separate from a ChatGPT or Claude subscription). If they fail, Gemini writes it.

      Jo's voice

      End-word mode: talk, pause, think, carry on. Jo sends when you say "Jo" again or another end word, like "Jo, send Ravi the rate list and remind me tomorrow, Jo", or when you tap the mic. The end word itself is left out. After 12 seconds of silence Jo stops and puts what you said in the box for you to send. In any mode: say "hold on" to keep Jo listening through a long pause, or "cancel that" to drop what you said.

      Jo Office waits this long after you stop talking (twice as long if you trail off with "and…", "the…"). Raise it if it still answers too early. Tap the mic to send at once.

      Mail senders are added automatically. Misheard anyway? Say "no, I said …", or click your message in the conversation to fix it and send again. Jo Office remembers the fix from then on.
